## About Me

<img align="right" src="https://profile-assets-d2o.pages.dev/flower-rounded.png" width="205" alt="White & Lavender Flower" />

Hi! I'm **Rohan Singh Bhandari**, a Full-Stack Developer from India building web platforms, scalable backends, and AI systems.

- **Full-Stack & Backend:** Building connected web apps and type-safe APIs with TypeScript, Next.js, and NestJS.
- **AI & Machine Learning:** Running, loading, and fine-tuning open-source models using Python, PyTorch, Transformers, Hugging Face, Scikit-learn, AI Agents, and MCP.
- **Cloud & DevOps:** Deploying and automating workflows across AWS, Cloudflare, Docker, CI/CD, Vercel, Render, and Railway.
- **Databases:** Designing relational and document schemas with NeonDB, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB.
- **Focus:** Turning complex ideas into practical products people actually use.

> *Learn consistently. Build patiently. Ship things that work.*
